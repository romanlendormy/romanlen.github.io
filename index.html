Learning Chance-Constrained Bounds

01 · ML FOR COMBINATORIAL OPTIMIZATION · 03 → 07 2026
Research internship · CORAIL Research Group, Polytechnique Montréal

Chance-constrained problems require constraints to hold with high probability rather than deterministically. Sampling scenarios from observations turns this stochastic problem into a deterministic one, which is easier to solve. We propose a general approach to solve any combinatorial chance-constrained problem with branch and bound. In our algorithm, bounds are derived from a Lagrangian decomposition over the scenarios, typically tightened through a sub-gradient ascent. We replace this expensive optimization with a graph neural network that predicts the multipliers in a single forward pass, trained self-supervised on the bound it induces, which remains valid regardless of the precision of the GNN. On the chance-constrained multi-dimensional knapsack: about 10× fewer explored nodes and up to 2× faster solving on the largest instances vs. a constraint-programming baseline.

Sketch Me That Shoe

02 · GENERATIVE MODELS · COMPUTER VISION · 02 → 03 2026
Polytechnique course mini-project

Reimplements fine-grained sketch-based retrieval (Yu et al., CVPR 2016) and extends it to generation: a sketch mapper inverts sketches into StyleGAN2's W+ space, supervised by a photo-mapper teacher and the frozen retrieval network, with a pix2pix baseline for comparison. Every model was rebuilt and retrained from scratch.

Purchase prediction with Carrefour

RECOMMENDER SYSTEMS · 09 2024 → 04 2025
Polytechnique team project, with Carrefour

Predicting which products a Carrefour customer will buy next, from purchase history and product features. We compared weighted S-POP, CatBoost, a simple neural network, RNNs and transformers. Best HitRate@10: CatBoost (0.384) and the neural network (0.370).
